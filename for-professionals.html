Founder Vision

A Vision for Global Heritage

"Our long-term vision is to have IPF certified schools on every continent, teaching natural perfumery in every language - ensuring this heritage is never lost."

- Creezy Courtoy, IPF Chairman & Founder of the Teacher's Academy

Creezy Courtoy founded the International Perfume Foundation (IPF) in 1995 in Paris. She is the author of The New Luxury Code and has dedicated three decades to preserving and transmitting the heritage of natural perfumery worldwide.

How It Works

1

Step 1 - Contact Us

Send us a brief overview of your current teaching practice or school. We'll arrange an initial Zoom consultation.

2

Step 2 - Assessment

We review your curriculum and experience against IPF standards. For Teacher Certification, this includes a pedagogical evaluation.

3

Step 3 - Certification

Upon approval, you receive your IPF certification, are listed in the global directory, and gain access to the IPF teacher network.

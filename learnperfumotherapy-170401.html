Overview

Program Overview

Perfumotherapy brings together three disciplines - Olfaction Training, Natural Aromatherapy and Natural Perfumery - into a single professional path. After successfully completing all courses, you can apply to become a Certified Perfumotherapist.

Follow an 18-week intensive online course: 4 weeks of Olfaction Training (2 modules/week), 4 weeks of Natural Aromatherapy (2 modules/week) and 10 weeks of Natural Perfumery (1 module/week). Olfaction Training and Aromatherapy can be taken simultaneously, reducing the length of the course to 14 weeks.

On completion you receive 4 certificates with a QR Code from the IPF, and your name, contact details and picture are listed in the IPF Directory as one of the first Perfumotherapists.

Our Mission

Protecting a Disappearing Olfactory Heritage

Natural perfumery is a heritage that stretches back millennia. A living tradition shaped by centuries of botanical knowledge, artisanal precision and cultural memory. For over three decades, the IPF has stood as a quiet but determined counterpoint to the industrial standardisation of fragrance.

Our mission is the transmission of this heritage to a new generation of perfumers, teachers and brand founders, ensuring that the language of natural materials, ethical sourcing and authentic composition is not lost to time or to mass production.

The Study Experience

How Studying from Home Actually Works

Natural perfumery is a sensory craft. Study-from-home does not mean studying alone. It means structured, mentored learning that fits your life, wherever you are.

01

Enrol & Meet Your Teacher

Once enrolled, a personal Zoom meeting is arranged with your instructor before the course begins. You are never just a number.

02

Study at Your Own Pace

Each week, a new module is released. Written lessons, practical exercises and video guidance are available on your own schedule, from anywhere in the world.

03

Personal Mentorship Throughout

Your instructor reviews your work and provides individual feedback. Consultancies remain available after course completion to support your professional journey.

04

Receive Your IPF Certificate

Upon successful completion, you receive your internationally recognised IPF certificate with QR verification, and are listed in the global directory of certified perfumers.

Program Overview

Terry Johnson has worked with hundreds of businesses, helping them with the business, marketing and branding fundamentals necessary to succeed even against the strongest competition. This course is part of the IPF Natural Perfumery Teacher's Academy Curriculum.

Completing this course, you will be able to:

  • Renew your marketing, business and branding for a post-pandemic world
  • Understand basic marketing terms and fundamentals
  • Identify, build and market post-pandemic brand value
  • Re-imagine your mission, vision and value proposition
  • Write a winning business plan and protect your business

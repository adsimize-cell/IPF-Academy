Overview

Program Overview

With this course, you can become a Certified World Perfume History Teacher. Perfumers or brands gain a better understanding of the importance of each country's perfume heritage - which may prevent failing a product launch, as each country has its own history and use of fragrances.

What works in the Western world may not work in the Middle East or in the East, where countries such as China and Japan have different olfactory senses and different ways to use perfumes. This course will also inspire you by giving you thousands of storytelling opportunities to use in social media. The history of perfume is also the story of famous women and men through the lens of perfume.

A synoptic table accompanies the first module, to help you understand the use and evolution of perfumery through the ages in different parts of the world. A personal Zoom meeting is organised before the course, and a survey is sent afterwards.

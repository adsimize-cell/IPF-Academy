Overview

Program Overview

With these master classes, perfumers or brands gain a better understanding of the importance of each country's perfume heritage - which may prevent failing a product launch, as each country has its own history and use of fragrances. What works in the Western world may not work in the Middle East or the East, where China and Japan have different olfactory senses and ways to use perfumes.

Each master class will also inspire you by giving you thousands of storytelling opportunities to use in social media. The history of perfume is also the story of famous women and men through the lens of perfume.

Take the masterclasses individually - 250€ per module - or follow the complete World Perfume History Course.

Overview

Become a Certified Teacher

This intensive Teacher Masterclass on Teaching Methodology is created and given by Creezy Courtoy, IPF Chairman and Founder of the Teacher's Academy. With this course, you will learn the instructional techniques necessary to teach Natural Perfumery Courses and Masterclasses. You will also learn how to transmit the value and excellence of the Natural Perfumery Teacher's Academy.

A Personal Zoom Meeting is organised before the course. An Annual Review Test will be made each year before re-certification.

After finishing the course with success, you will be listed as a Certified Natural Perfumery Teacher in the IPF Directory.

Featured Exhibition

Les Routes du Parfum

Les Routes du Parfum - The Routes of Perfume - traces the journeys of scent across cultures and centuries, presenting some pieces of the Courtoy Collection.

The exhibition invites visitors to rediscover perfume as heritage: an art rooted in agriculture, culture and the soul of flowers.

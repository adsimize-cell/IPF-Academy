Natural Perfumery Luxury Advanced Private Mentoring

A personal Natural Perfumery program

A bespoke, one-to-one educational and creative engagement designed for individuals seeking advanced refinement rather than foundational instruction. Why This Mentorship Exists

Judgment, Not Formulas

Natural perfumery is often taught through fragmented lenses: either as a technical craft stripped of artistic rigor, or as an expressive practice disconnected from material discipline. This mentorship exists to reunite material intelligence, authorship and ethical responsibility, based on the New Luxury Code and the IPF's philosophy and ethics.

The program unfolds through direct transmission, sustained critical dialogue and continuous practical work. Each mentorship is shaped around the participant's background, artistic vision and professional objectives, allowing for depth, precision and intellectual freedom.

This mentorship is practice-led: every concept is explored through direct work with materials, accords, bases and finished compositions, so that thinking and making evolve together.

How It Is Conducted

The Atelier Format

  • All sessions are online, live and strictly one-to-one - no group classes or prerecorded material
  • Weekly 60-minute private sessions: review of practical work, technical or conceptual deepening, targeted refinements
  • Hands-on work with 50+ curated natural raw materials; construction and refinement of accords, bases and complete compositions
  • Iterative testing, dilution, rebalancing and editing; all practical work is personally reviewed and refined
  • Independent work between sessions is essential and expected

What This Mentorship Is Not

For Long-Term Mastery

This program is not designed to optimise products for mass-market trends, to deliver shortcuts, recipes or proprietary formulas, or to accelerate output at the expense of integrity.

It exists for those committed to long-term mastery, not immediacy - a luxury atelier experience defined by time, attention and discernment. Its outcome: a shift from makers of fragrances to authors of olfactory work.

Acceptance Criteria

Admission by Application Only

Admission is selective, based on alignment, readiness and potential rather than formal credentials alone. A background in perfumery is not mandatory, but relevant experience in perfumery, art, design or material-based practices will be evaluated. Applicants should demonstrate:

◆A serious interest in fragrance as a material, artistic or cultural practice

◆Sensitivity to materials, form and sensory nuance

◆An emerging or established personal vision, even if still evolving

◆Openness to critique, refinement and reduction

◆Ability to work independently and take responsibility for the process

◆An ethical approach to materials and making

Applicants may be asked to submit a brief statement of intent, an overview of their practice, examples of work when relevant, and a short consultation call.

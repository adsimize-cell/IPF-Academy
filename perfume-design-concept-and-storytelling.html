Overview

Program Overview

This masterclass focuses on giving a graphic form to your ideas and inspirations. Together we analyse successful storytelling in the world of fragrances, learn how to create moodboards, and practise translating those moodboards into accords and fragrances.

The class is intensive and suitable for advanced beginners, intermediate and advanced students. After registration, the list of raw materials to be purchased will be sent to you.

After completing the class successfully, you receive a certificate from the International Perfume Foundation.

Overview

Program Overview

This is an intensive Study From Home program, part of the International Perfume Foundation Curriculum. The QR Code on the certificate is linked to the IPF's certification page. Personal attention is given to each student and consultancies are available after the courses. Our objective is to help you build a successful business - brand, teaching, independent perfumer.

The Master Natural Perfumery program requires completion of three core courses:

  • Natural Perfumery - Foundational botanical perfumery training
  • Business & Marketing - Build and market your fragrance brand
  • World Perfume History - Cultural heritage of perfumery from antiquity to today

Possibility to pay in 3 or 4 instalments.

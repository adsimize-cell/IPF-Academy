Our Difference

What Sets Us Apart

At our Natural Perfumery Academy, we stand out by nurturing and certifying genuine Natural Perfumers. We aim to expand the natural perfume market, catering to discerning consumers seeking authentic natural products to preserve their well-being.

Our comprehensive curriculum includes in-depth explorations of natural essences from aromatherapy to French natural perfumery, perfume history, the business of perfumery, and over ten specialised courses complemented by more than 35 courses and masterclasses. Each course and masterclass is taught by an international expert in their field.

What differentiates the Natural Perfumery Academy is that we emphasise the art of perfumery and prioritise academic and technical education to make each certified IPF perfumer a professional profile.

Once the perfumer has graduated, they have the opportunity to be fully supported by our international experts with coaching services: fragrance development and production in Grasse (made in France), business and marketing, supply chain, and packaging.

Our Commitment to You

Standards

We guide our students in crafting their unique perfumes, adhering to professional formulas while upholding the New Luxury Code, the standards of the International Perfume Foundation, and international safety regulations.

Independence

Beyond education, we are dedicated to empowering our students to develop their brands and succeed in the competitive world of perfumery.

Heritage

Natural perfumery is a heritage stretching back millennia. Today, this historic knowledge-base is in danger. We exist to protect it.

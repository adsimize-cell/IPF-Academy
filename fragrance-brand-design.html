Overview

Program Overview

This course is given by Pinar Lacroix, who brings together her experience in brand design systems and packaging design to empower entrepreneurs to develop a timeless visual identity for their fragrances without breaking the bank. The course teaches scalable design strategies and systems that align with emotional storytelling and natural perfumery values.

By the end of this course, you will be equipped to build a system for your brand legacy, create lasting emotional stories, and scale your fragrance brand.

Course objectives:

  • Build a cohesive and professional brand identity for your fragrance.
  • Explore the psychology of branding - how emotions and storytelling influence consumer choices and build loyalty.
  • Design mood boards, names, logos, packaging and visual systems aligned with your brand identity.
  • Create scalable design foundations that can evolve into custom bottles and physical packaging.
